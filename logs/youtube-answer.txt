To add a YouTube video to your digital signs, follow these steps:

* **Go to Apps:** Log in to your OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select the YouTube App:** Search for "YouTube" and click on the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Video Details:** Paste your video link into the **URL** field (supports regular links, share links, and YouTube Shorts) and enter an optional **Name**.
* **Adjust Settings (Optional):** Expand **Display options** to toggle captions or mute the video, or use **Playback options** to set custom Start At and End At times.
* **Save and Assign:** Click **Save**. You can now assign the video directly to your screens or add it to a playlist.
